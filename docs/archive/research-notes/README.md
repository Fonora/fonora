# Research notes, archived

> **No longer maintained, and no longer part of the project's documentation.**
> The language is described by five living documents: the [rulebook](../../fonoran-rulebook.md),
> the three algorithm pages ([roots](../../fonoran-algorithm-roots.md),
> [compounds](../../fonoran-algorithm-compounds.md),
> [translation](../../fonoran-algorithm-translation.md)), and the
> [architecture map](../../fonoran-architecture.md).

These 38 notes are the narrative record of how the project got here between June and July 2026.
They are kept as history. They are **not** a source of truth: several describe decisions that
were later reversed (LLM-generated vocabulary, the pattern-matching English parser, questions
without a particle), and nothing in the codebase reads them.

Links inside these notes point at `/research/notes/<slug>` routes and at documents that have
since moved or been deleted. Those links are dead on purpose; the runtime that served them
was removed in the same change that archived these files.
